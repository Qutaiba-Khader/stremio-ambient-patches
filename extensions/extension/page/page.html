The colours along the picture's edges go to HyperHDR while Stremio plays. Every change applies at once and is saved on the TV.

HyperHDR

HyperHDR address (its flatbuffer server; port 19400 when left out)
In HyperHDR the lower number wins.

Points

Distance and depth are % of the picture's height. Width is how much of its share of the edge each area covers.

Colour

HDR correction reads the picture as an HDR (PQ) signal and brings it down to SDR levels. Use it when HDR films look pale or too bright on the lights.

Playback

Off by default. With it on, the video goes straight to the screen and never reaches the app, so the lights get nothing. Takes effect from the next video. Audio passthrough works either way.
Takes effect the next time Stremio starts.
