HDR light

An HDR picture reaches the app as light in nits. These choose how much light is full LED white and how much LED range the highlights above it get.

Lower the white level for brighter lights. 203 nits is HDR's reference white. With headroom, white lights the LEDs below full, so brighter highlights still stand out.
